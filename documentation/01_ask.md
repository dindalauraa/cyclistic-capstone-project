# Cyclistic Bike-Share Case Study
## Ask Phase

### 1. Business Task

Analyze 12 months of Cyclistic historical bike-trip data, from January 1, 2025, to December 31, 2025, to identify differences in how casual riders and annual members use the service. The findings will help the marketing team identify usage patterns that may inform strategies to encourage casual riders to purchase annual memberships.

### 2. Business Questions

Three questions guide Cyclistic's future marketing program:

1. How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?
2. Why would casual riders purchase Cyclistic annual memberships?
3. How can Cyclistic use digital media to influence casual riders to become members?

**Primary research question:** How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?

This analysis focuses on the first question. The other two questions provide context for the broader marketing program but are not directly addressed by this analysis.

### 3. Stakeholders

- **Lily Moreno, Director of Marketing:** Uses the findings to inform marketing strategies aimed at increasing annual memberships.
- **Cyclistic Marketing Analytics Team:** Analyzes trip data and communicates insights to support marketing decisions.
- **Cyclistic Executive Team:** Reviews the proposed marketing program and evaluates recommendations for implementation.

### 4. Decision Supported

The analysis will help the marketing team understand how casual riders and annual members differ in their use of Cyclistic bikes. These insights can inform potential marketing opportunities and support the development of targeted strategies for converting casual riders into annual members.

The findings describe observed usage patterns; they do not independently establish customer motivations or guarantee that a marketing strategy will increase memberships.
